The truth is, real estate trends are local—what happens in your neighborhood has a bigger impact on your home’s value than global news. If you’re curious about your local market’s stability, take a look around:

  • Well-maintained properties suggest a strong market.
  • Signs of neglect (vacant homes, overgrown yards, deferred maintenance) may indicate a decline in home values.

What History Tells Us About Market Fluctuations

Looking back at 2008, we saw:

  • A significant drop in sales volume (about 50% fewer transactions in the months following the financial crisis).
  • A similar trend today, with some areas reporting up to 70% fewer sales compared to previous years.
  • Stable prices because both supply and demand have dropped.
  • A shift toward a buyer’s market, putting downward pressure on high-end property prices.

What’s Next for the Real Estate Market?

As the economy reopens, we expect:

  • A surge of new listings from sellers who originally planned to move.
  • Buyers who are hesitant or financially limited, leading to less competition.
  • A strong buyer’s market in many regions, creating great opportunities for those ready to buy.

Should You Sell Now or Wait?

Deciding whether to sell now or later depends on your personal situation and risk tolerance. Some sellers are choosing to:

  • List now and secure top dollar with a long closing period to buy later when inventory increases.
  • Utilize virtual real estate tools to attract serious buyers while minimizing in-person interactions.

]]> /the-billion-dollar-question-where-is-the-real-estate-market-headed/../../feed/index.html 0 How to Build the Perfect Wishlist for Your Future Home /how-to-build-the-perfect-wishlist-for-your-future-home/ /how-to-build-the-perfect-wishlist-for-your-future-home/#respond Mon, 10 Feb 2025 06:07:26 +0000 /?p=7026 Finding the right home is about more than just price and location—it’s about discovering a space that fits your lifestyle, needs, and long-term goals. If you’re planning to buy in the future, now is the perfect time to create a home wishlist to guide your search. Follow these four steps to define what truly matters in your next home.

1) Analyze Your Current Home: Likes and Dislikes

Before you start imagining your dream home, take a closer look at where you live now.

  • Rate your home on a scale from 1 to 10 based on comfort, functionality, and overall satisfaction.
  • Identify the best and worst features—from layout and design to storage and lighting.
  • Consider if the style and architecture match your taste, or if you prefer something different.
  • Think about your favorite space in your home—what makes it special?

Understanding what works (and what doesn’t) will help shape your vision for the perfect home.

2) Define Your Space and Layout Needs

Your home should complement your daily routine and lifestyle. Ask yourself:

  • Is the layout practical for your needs? What would you change?
  • Bedrooms & Bathrooms: Do you have enough, or would you prefer more?
  • Outdoor Space: Do you enjoy maintaining a yard, or would a smaller space be better?
  • Amenities: Would you like extras like a home office, gym, or entertainment area?
  • Curb Appeal & Parking: How would you rate your home’s exterior? Is the driveway or garage sufficient?

3) Consider Your Ideal Neighbourhood

A great home is also about the community around it. Think about:

  • Neighbourhood atmosphere: Do you value friendly neighbours and a strong sense of community?
  • Proximity to essentials: Are grocery stores, parks, and schools conveniently located?
  • Work commute: Are you satisfied with your daily travel time?

4) Set a Realistic Budget

Understanding your financial comfort zone will help narrow down your search:

  • Are your current housing costs manageable?
  • If buying a new home, how much of an increase (if any) could you comfortably afford?
  • Would a lower monthly payment better suit your financial situation?

Be Ready When the Right Home Comes Along

A well-thought-out wishlist will help you make informed decisions when you’re ready to enter the market.
