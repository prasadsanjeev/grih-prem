Taking the time to clean and reorganize your fridge can help you save time, reduce waste, and even cut down on grocery costs. Here’s a simple step-by-step guide to keeping your fridge neat and functional.

1) Start Fresh – Clear Out Your Fridge

The first step to a well-organized fridge is to empty it completely. Check all food items and toss anything that’s expired or past its prime. Leftovers should generally be discarded after a few days, and old vegetables or meat should be removed. Once everything is out, give the interior a thorough wipe-down to remove any spills or lingering odors.

2) Use Washable Refrigerator Mats for Easy Cleanup

One of the easiest ways to keep your fridge clean for longer is by using washable refrigerator mats. These mats catch spills, prevent stains, and make cleaning a breeze—simply remove, wash, and place them back in. They’re a great way to cut down on deep cleaning sessions and keep your fridge looking fresh.

3) Organize with Storage Bins

If your fridge often feels like a jumbled mess, storage bins can be a game-changer. Group similar items together in clear bins to create a more organized space. For example, keep dairy products in one bin, condiments in another, and fresh produce in a separate section. This system makes it easier to find what you need and prevents food from getting lost at the back.

4) Keep Healthy Snacks Within Reach

For families with kids, placing pre-washed fruits and vegetables at the front of the fridge encourages healthy snacking. When nutritious options are easy to grab, it makes it more likely that your family will reach for them instead of less healthy alternatives.

5) Stay on Top of Expiration Dates

It’s easy for expired food to get pushed to the back of the fridge and forgotten. Make it a habit to check expiration dates regularly. As a general rule:

  • Fish: 1–2 days
  • Meat: 2–3 days
  • Vegetables & Grains: 5–7 days

By keeping an eye on these dates, you’ll reduce food waste and avoid unpleasant surprises.

6) Rotate Older Items to the Front

One of the best ways to prevent waste is to use the “first in, first out” rule. When you bring home new groceries, move the older items to the front and place the newer ones behind them. This simple trick helps ensure that nothing goes bad before you get the chance to use it.

A Well-Organized Fridge for a Smoother Kitchen Experience

Following these steps will help you maintain a clean, efficient, and organized refrigerator, making meal prep easier and reducing unnecessary food waste.

Whether you’re looking for thrilling outdoor experiences, wildlife encounters, or a glimpse into history, there’s something for everyone. Here are some fantastic day trips to enjoy with your loved ones this summer.

1) Discover Wildlife at Greenview Aviaries Park & Zoo

Just 30 minutes from Chatham, Greenview Aviaries Park & Zoo is an excellent spot for a family-friendly outing. The park is home to a variety of animals, including tigers, lions, zebras, and wolves, as well as an array of exotic birds. After exploring the zoo, kids can burn off energy at the playground while parents relax in the picnic area. It’s the perfect combination of fun and learning for all ages!

2) Relax and Explore at Pinery Provincial Park

Nestled along the shores of Lake Huron, Pinery Provincial Park is a nature lover’s paradise. With 10 km of sandy beaches, unique ecosystems, and over 300 species of birds, this park offers breathtaking scenery and endless outdoor activities. Families can rent canoes, kayaks, or paddleboats, enjoy the 14 km biking trail, or take a scenic walk along one of the many hiking trails. To top it off, Pinery’s sunsets have been named among the Top 10 Best in the World by National Geographic!

3) Experience Nature’s Beauty at Hilton Falls Conservation Area

For those who love the outdoors, Hilton Falls Conservation Area, located near Milton, is a must-visit destination. The park features stunning waterfalls, peaceful rivers, and historic mill ruins. Whether you’re into hiking, biking, or simply enjoying a peaceful stroll through nature, this spot offers a perfect escape from the city.

4) Dive into History at the Museum of Naval History

If you’re looking for an indoor adventure, the Museum of Naval History provides a fascinating glimpse into Canada’s maritime past. Located an hour south of London, the museum is home to the HMCS OJIBWA, a Cold War-era submarine. Families can step inside and explore the 90-meter-long vessel while learning about its journey from the ocean to Ontario.

Create Lasting Memories with Your Family

A day trip is a great way to unwind, explore new places, and create cherished family moments. With so many incredible destinations, Ontario has no shortage of adventures waiting for you.
